How Infopedia is written

  1. Draft from primary data, peer-reviewed literature and archival records.
  2. Cite every empirical claim inline, with a link to the original.
  3. Review disputed points against independent sources, and say where evidence conflicts.
  4. Correct continuously — readers can propose edits on any article.
